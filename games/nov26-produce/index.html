Peas in the pod

Everyone starts with five peas. If a card is true for you, tap a pea to eat it. Tap again to grow it back.

    About the book

    Pounded by Produce: A Veggie Love Tale is a comedic romance novella by G. M. Fairy, the author of the Get In My Swamp series.

    Emily is running from a rough past and lands a kitchen job at a quiet countryside parish. Her new neighbors are Robert and Laurent, best friends who became priests. After a night under the harvest moon, they wake up as a tomato and a cucumber, and keep switching between human and vegetable form.

    Emily, the priests, and a whole lot of temptation end up tangled together, with vows on the line. These cards borrow the themes, and nothing beyond the back-cover blurb.

    A fan-made party game for adults. Cheeky, never explicit. Not affiliated with G. M. Fairy or the publisher.

    How to play

    Four quick steps, then pass the phone around.

    1. 1 Add your playersScroll down to “Peas in the pod” and type each name. Everyone starts with five peas. Prefer fingers? Skip this.
    2. 2 Choose your decksLeave all four on for the full mix, or switch off any you don’t want.
    3. 3 Draw a cardTap the big card and read it out loud to the group.
    4. 4 Own upIf it’s true for you, tap one of your peas to eat it. The last player with peas wins. Harvest moon cards add a twist.
